# Recording Script 01 — Elena's First Letter

**Filename:** `elena-first-letter.mp3`  
**Voice:** Elena, Nora's mother  
**Format:** Read aloud as a personal letter  
**Target duration:** 1 minute 30 seconds–2 minutes  
**Mood:** Warm and sincere, with a slight ache beneath the hope. Do not sound theatrical.

## Recording directions
- Begin gently, as if reading a letter written to a beloved child.
- Pause briefly after the greeting and between paragraphs.
- Keep the voice natural; do not overact sadness.
- Read the six target phrases clearly because students will reconstruct them from context.
- Record the full version in one uninterrupted take. We will create the six-blank learner version on the website.

## Full letter

Dear Nora,

I hope this letter finds you well and that you are still keeping your little notebook of drawings. I often imagine you sitting by the window, making up stories about the people who pass by.

I know leaving was difficult for both of us. I took this job overseas because I wanted to give you a safer home and more opportunities when you grow up. I thought that if I worked hard now, you would have more choices later. But I also know that money cannot take the place of a mother's arms, and I miss you more than I can explain.

Please remember that **I have not forgotten you**, even when the days become busy and the distance feels enormous. I write whenever I can, and I keep every letter you send me. Your words are small treasures that make this unfamiliar place feel less lonely.

If a letter takes longer than expected, **please do not think I have stopped caring**. I will keep trying to reach you. I hope you will keep writing to me, too, even if all you have to tell me is what you ate for breakfast or what made you laugh at school.

I wish I could be there for every important day. Until then, tell me about your dreams, your friends, and the things you are proud of. I want to know the person you are becoming, one letter at a time.

With all my love,

Mum

## Six listening targets
1. I have not forgotten you
2. I took this job overseas
3. money cannot take the place of a mother's arms
4. please do not think I have stopped caring
5. I will keep trying to reach you
6. one letter at a time

**Important story note:** This is an early letter written when Nora is younger, before the Grade XI music competition. The evidence in the box will establish its date and show that at least some letters were returned or affected by address changes. The letter expresses Elena's intention, but by itself it does not prove that every letter reached Nora.

The Forgotten Box

Read the briefing carefully. Every detail may matter.

CASE BRIEFING CONFIDENTIAL

While helping her family pack up their old home, Nora, a Grade XI student, discovers a dusty box hidden behind a stack of family albums. Inside are old envelopes, handwritten letters, and a photograph of her mother, Elena.

Elena left to work overseas when Nora was five, promising to keep in touch through letters. Over the years, their communication became irregular. Nora grew convinced that her mother had stopped trying to be part of her life.

Then Nora finds letters written by both of them—some dated around the very years she remembers feeling forgotten. Several envelopes carry different addresses. One bears a postal return mark.

Your assignment: find out what the evidence can tell us—and what it cannot.
